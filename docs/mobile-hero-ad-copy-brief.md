# Mobile Hero Ad Copy — Brief for Approval

## Objective

Create ad copy that brings qualified business owners to the S-Corp savings estimator, where they can receive a directional estimate of potential tax savings.

## Audience

Business owners who suspect they may be overpaying taxes or operating with an inefficient entity structure. The current homepage also speaks to owners seeking proactive tax strategy, bookkeeping, and fractional CFO support.

## Approved facts available to use

- Union National Tax offers an interactive S-Corp savings estimator.
- The estimator asks business-owner-focused questions and returns a directional estimate.
- The firm has an IRS Enrolled Agent credential and 15+ years of experience.

## Primary action

Start the S-Corp savings estimator.

## Secondary action

Explore Union National Tax services.

## Working positioning

Lead with the concrete question of whether the visitor may be overpaying, then clarify that the assessment concerns S-Corp structure and potential savings rather than promising a universal tax review or a guaranteed result.

## Decisions needed before ad copy is drafted

- Ad channel and format: paid social, paid search, display, email, or another placement.
- Audience segment and geography.
- Whether "IRS Enrolled Agent" may be paired with an explanatory claim such as federal IRS representation.
- Whether the proposed hero hierarchy—"Stop Overpaying the IRS." followed by "Build a Smarter Business."—is approved for use in advertising.

## Assumptions

- Audience is problem-aware and evaluating tax-planning options.
- The estimator is the desired conversion event, rather than a discovery-call booking.
- No savings guarantees, unsupported statistics, or client claims will be used.
