## Rationale

- The variants lead with a specific problem, then name the actual interactive destination.
- “Directional estimate” avoids implying a guaranteed tax outcome.
- The credential is used as proof of expertise, not as a claim of a particular result.
- CTA language is specific to the S-Corp estimator, avoiding the broader promise implied by “See If You're Overpaying.”
