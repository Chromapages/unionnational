# Testimonial presentation policy

## Decision

The dark Before / After / Outcome card is a featured result, not a third testimonial-card variant. It intentionally leads the section and summarizes a curated business transformation. White cards are direct client quotes and retain their distinct quote-and-attribution format.

## Why this mix is intentional

A featured result provides a fast, structured proof point, while quotes provide individual voice and credibility. Making every quote conform to a case-study template would require details that are not always available or appropriate to disclose.

## Scale rule

Render at most one featured result per testimonial section. It always appears first. Follow it with quote-only testimonials that meet the attribution publication rule (name plus role-and-company, or a verified-client label). Additional testimonials extend the quote list; they do not introduce another format or a second featured-result card.

## Future case studies

When sufficient client data is available, promote a quote to the featured-result slot only after collecting documented Before, After, and Outcome fields. Do not present partially collected case-study data in a quote card.
